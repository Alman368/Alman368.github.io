  1. Membership inference in tabular foundation models

    Models like TabPFN predict from private records placed in their context. Even against the published defense, an attacker can tell which records were there almost perfectly (0.87–1.00). My defense, SAG‑Tab, lowers that in 144 of 144 comparisons, to about 0.6, for 0.2–3.8 accuracy points.

    2026, ongoing | bachelor's thesis, supervised by Leonardo Franco | python, pytorch | paper in preparation
    Inferencia de pertenencia en modelos fundacionales tabulares
